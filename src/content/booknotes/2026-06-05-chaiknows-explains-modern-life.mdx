## Why This Name Works

The original file name is useful for the pipeline, but it does not tell a reader what the book is about. `Chaiknows Explains Modern Life` is clearer because the collection is not limited to one topic. It explains the hidden systems behind phones, AI, cars, health, internet services, consumer products, money, media, and daily-life decisions.

## Overview

This book is a broad science and technology reader built from Chaiknows video transcripts. The strongest theme is practical explanation: each chapter starts from a familiar question and then opens up the engineering, business, medical, legal, or social mechanism behind it.

The collection is especially useful for Chinese reading practice because it mixes everyday language with technical vocabulary. Many chapters also include bilingual titles, which makes the book useful for building English terms around technology and science.

## How To Study It

1. Pick one explainer based on a real question you already care about.
2. Write the core question in your own words before reading.
3. Extract five technical terms and five everyday phrases from the chapter.
4. Summarize the mechanism in three sentences: what happens, why it happens, and why it matters.
5. Save one sentence pattern that could be reused in English or Chinese writing.
