## Background & Overview

Record the background, timeline, destinations, and travel companions for this trip:
- **Travel Dates**: Month Day, Year – Month Day, Year
- **Destinations**: Cities / National Parks / Trails
- **Companions**: Family / Friends / Solo

---

## Highlights & Itinerary

Outline the route or key itinerary milestones:
1. **Day 1**: Arrival, check-in, casual stroll, and local dinner.
2. **Day 2**: Exploring attractions, hiking trails, or sampling regional cuisine.
3. **Day 3**: Final visits and the journey home.

---

## Observations & Notes

### 1. Landscapes and Local Vibe
Describe the terrain, city architecture, nature, and the overall atmosphere.

> Tip: To include local photos saved within this bundle (e.g. `01.jpg` or `featured.jpg`), reference them directly with:
> `![Photo caption](01.jpg)`

### 2. Dining and Experiences
Memorable meals, local spots, or unique activities that stood out.

### 3. Practical Tips (Optional)
- **Transport / Rental**: Driving conditions and logistics.
- **Lodging**: Campsites or hotels worth recommending.
- **Gear**: Weather considerations, footwear, and essentials.

---

## Reflections & Takeaways

Travel provides a welcomed break from routine. Capture the moments that lingered in your memory, along with any quiet reflections from time on the road.
